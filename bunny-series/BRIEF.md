## Spine **[claude]**

Through-line: *a small rabbit plants a seed that fell from the moon, and learning to care for it
teaches Pip to share.* Token: **the seed** — a pale glowing seed in ep 1, a sprout, a stalk, a
moon-flower, and in ep 5 a new seed drifting off-screen to someone else.

| Ep | Title | Setup → transformation → payoff |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Seed | Pip watches the moon from the meadow; a glowing seed falls into the grass; Pip digs a hole, plants it, waters it — at dawn a tiny sprout. *alone → caretaker* |
| 2 | The Storm | Rain and wind threaten the sprout; Pip holds a big dock leaf over it all night; the sky clears and the sprout has grown a second leaf. *fear → brave* |
| 3 | The Visitor | A hungry hedgehog sniffs at the plant; Pip almost chases it away, then shares a carrot instead; two friends sit by the stalk. *mine → ours* |
| 4 | The Climb | The stalk is now taller than the hedge; at night Pip climbs it with the hedgehog watching; from the top, the whole world and the moon close up. *small → big world* |
| 5 | The Flower | The bud opens into a glowing moon-flower that lights the meadow; animals gather; Pip blows its new seeds into the wind — one floats toward the moon. *receive → give* |

Each episode: 3 s hook (no blank holds), title card lettered by hand early, a turn caused in
the world, a resolve on a downbeat and ~2 s to breathe, "next time" token at the end.
